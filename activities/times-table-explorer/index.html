How to use this explorer
  1. Choose a table from 3 through 9. That number is the height of the rectangle.
  2. Choose the other factor from 1 through 12. That number is the width.
  3. When the width is 10, 11, or 12, the rectangle splits into a tens part and an ones part.
  4. Click a square, or press the arrow keys, to see and read its row and column.
