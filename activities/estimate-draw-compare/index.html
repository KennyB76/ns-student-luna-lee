Need a reminder?

Round halfway numbers up: 45 → 50. Compatible factors are nearby numbers that are easy to multiply together.

For positive factors: both increase → over; both decrease → under. If one stays the same, follow the changed factor. If both stay the same, the product is exact. If one increases and one decreases, direction alone cannot tell you.

Example: 76 × 19 = 700 + 630 + 60 + 54 = 1,444. Rounding gives 80 × 20 = 1,600, over by 156. Compatible factors 75 × 20 = 1,500 give an overestimate by 56.
