Student: Luna LeeDate: September 16, 2026
Standard: CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.4.NBT.A.2–3 — Read, write, compare, and round whole numbers.
Mr. Kenny's Key Point
For every rounding problem, draw and label the number line, find halfway, mark the number, and compare distances. At halfway, explain our agreement to choose the greater end.
A. Number forms and place value
1. Write 564,087 in expanded form.
2. Write 30,000 + 7,000 + 800 + 2 in standard form.
3. Write 100,002 in word form.
4. What is the value of the 6 in 268,041?
5. Write four hundred sixteen thousand, eighty-seven in standard form.
B. Compare and explain
6. Compare 38,675 ___ 9,100. Explain.
7. Compare 16,420 ___ 10,000 + 6,000 + 400 + 2.
8. Compare without adding: 34,593 + 6,781 ___ 34,593 + 6,609.
9. Find all digits: 23,489 < 2□,650 < 26,472.
10. Population means the number of people living in a place. Use this textbook data: St. Louis 319,294; Anchorage 291,826; Oakland 390,724. Which TWO OTHER cities have populations not greater than Oakland? What does “not greater than” mean?
