NEWTON SCHOOL · LUNA'S CLASS NOTES

Population means people.

Population is the number of people who live in a place. A city's population tells us how many people live inside that city's boundaries.

We count people—babies, children, and adults. Population does not mean the number of buildings or the size of the land.

Imagine a tiny town.

A visitor who comes for a day does not become a resident just by visiting. A resident is someone who lives there.

2. More people → a longer bar

Seoul: about 9,600,000 people. Busan: about 3,300,000 people.
9,600,000 > 3,300,000. Seoul has more people than Busan—roughly three times as many.

The Korean values are rounded: Seoul's reported 2025 total of 9,579,177 rounds to 9,600,000; Busan's city introduction reports about 3.3 million for 2025. U.S. textbook values come from an older, unspecified year. This is a number-size lesson, not a same-year statistical comparison.

3. Compare the population numbers

Greater than: >

More people live there.

Tulsa: 391,906 > St. Louis: 319,294.

Not greater than: ≤

Less than OR equal to. It means “no more than.”

319,294 ≤ 390,724 is true.
390,724 ≤ 390,724 is also true.

Which cities are not greater than Oakland?

St. Louis (319,294) and Anchorage (291,826) both have fewer people than Oakland (390,724). Oakland itself is equal to Oakland, so it also meets ≤. If asked for two other cities, choose St. Louis and Anchorage.

Which two textbook cities are about equal?

Tulsa (391,906) and Oakland (390,724) differ by just 1,182 people. Their populations are close, but they are not exactly equal.

4. Tell me what you learned

Finish aloud: “Population means ____.”

Point to Anchorage. Is it in Alaska or in the main group of 48 states?

Point to Seoul and Busan. Which has the greater population?

Check your answers

Population means the number of people living in a place. Anchorage is in Alaska, which is part of the United States. Seoul has the greater population.
