11. A teacher says "seventy-three thousand, forty-six." Ava writes 73,460. Ben writes 73,046. Who is correct? Explain the other student's mistake.
1. Forty-six is 046 in the ones period. There are zero hundreds, four tens, and six ones.
2. Ava wrote 460, which means four hundred sixty.
Ben is correct: 73,046. Ava put the 4 and 6 one place too far left.

13. Lia says 300,000 + 5,000 + 20 + 6 is 35,026. Is she correct? Write the correct standard form and explain the role of the zeros.

2. Use 3 | 0 | 5 | 0 | 2 | 6; zero ten thousands and zero hundreds keep the other digits in place.
No. The correct number is 305,026.
